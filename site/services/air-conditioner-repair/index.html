Air Conditioner Repair

Air conditioners blowing warm, dripping indoors, running noisily, or short-cycling.

WhatsApp about this Call 0711 702 233

What usually goes wrong

An air conditioner that has quietly lost gas will keep running and keep costing you electricity while cooling almost nothing. Servicing catches that before the compressor takes the strain.

  • Blowing air that isn't cold
  • Water dripping from the indoor unit
  • Ice forming on the pipework
  • Rattling or grinding from the outdoor unit
  • Switching on and off every few minutes
  • A musty smell when it starts up
  • Remote or controls not responding

What we repair on it

  • Servicing, cleaning and filter work
  • Refrigerant leaks and recharging
  • Compressors and capacitors
  • Fan motors, indoor and outdoor
  • Blocked condensate drains
  • Control boards and sensors
  • Installation and relocation

How the repair goes

Step 1

You get in touch

Call or WhatsApp with the appliance, the brand if you know it, and what it's doing. A short description saves a wasted visit.

Step 2

We look at it

A technician comes to you, or you bring the appliance to the Mlolongo service centre. Diagnosis costs KSh 1,000.

Step 3

You get a price

We tell you what's wrong, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing. Nothing goes ahead until you say so.

Step 4

We do the work

On site where the job allows, back at the workshop where it needs a bench and parts.

Step 5

We test it with you

The appliance runs before we leave, so you see it working rather than taking our word for it.

Questions about air conditioner repair

What does air conditioner repair cost?

Diagnosis is KSh 1,000. The repair price depends on the fault and the parts, and we confirm it with you before any work starts.

Do you come to my home, or do I bring it in?

Both are available. Some faults are quicker to fix on a bench at our Mlolongo service centre, and we'll say so when we diagnose it.

Which areas do you cover for this?

Nairobi and Mlolongo, with home visits into Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu counties. Message us with your area and we'll confirm before you book.
